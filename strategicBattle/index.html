Field manual & faction stats

Capture every node to win. Losing all nodes and aircraft ends the mission. A deployment sends the selected percentage of whole population.

Damage = attackers × attack ÷ (defence + walls). Surviving attackers occupy a captured node. Walls remain after capture.

Village: 0.1 gold/s. City: 0.2. Mine: 0.5. Multiply by faction productivity. Towers and barracks produce no gold. Barracks generate no population. Only aircraft launched from barracks receive a 1.5× attack bonus, fixed at launch. Other nodes get no attack bonus. Other types grow at faction generation.

One owned tower grants all your nodes 1.2? defence; additional towers do not stack this bonus. Towers fire at the nearest hostile aircraft within 300 px, dealing 1 population damage per second. Each installation targets one aircraft, including hostile flights headed elsewhere. Neutral towers also defend their airspace.

Wall tier 1: 50 gold, +10 defence. Tier 2: 150 gold, another +20 defence (+30 total). Tier 3: 250 gold, adds a 1-DPS, 300-px laser with no extra defence. Three tiers maximum; a tower with tier 3 still has one laser. Type changes cost 60; cities require a 100-gold village upgrade. Each operation starts with 120 gold.
