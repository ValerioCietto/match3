Preventing gambling harm

  • Don't chase losses. More betting is not a reliable way to recover lost money.
  • Protect your essentials. Never borrow to gamble or use money needed for bills, food, or rent.
  • Set boundaries and take breaks. Decide time and spending limits in advance. Choosing not to gamble is always an option.
  • Notice the warning signs. Hiding gambling, feeling unable to stop, or gambling to escape distress are reasons to seek support.
  • Make support easier to reach. Talk to someone you trust or a health professional. Gambling blocks and self-exclusion can help create distance.
