Terms of heroism

Saving the world is unpaid.

Recruit an increasingly skeptical party, fight on a turn-based timeline, and follow a suspiciously convenient trail of regional bosses to the Dark Lord’s castle.

Time pauses while you choose. Merchants do not offer hero discounts. Monsters carry gold for reasons nobody can adequately explain.
